1. **User Registration & Role-Based Access :** Different users will have different permissions, including pet owners, veterinary clinics, rescue organizations, and ward/municipal officers.

2. **Pet Registration & Digital Pet Profile :** Pet owners can create a digital profile containing the pet’s photo, age, breed, gender, color, medical details, and vaccination history.

3. **AI Photo-Based Breed Finder:** Users can upload a pet image, and the system will predict the most likely breed or breed group.

4. **QR-Based Pet Identification:** Every registered pet receives a unique QR code linked to a public emergency profile containing selected identification, owner contact, and vaccination information.

5. **Veterinarian-Verified Vaccination Records:** Verified clinics can record vaccines, batch numbers, vaccination dates, booster dates, and other relevant medical details.

6. **Vaccination Tracking & Automated Reminders:** Vaccination status will automatically appear as Up to Date, Due Soon, or Overdue, with reminders sent before booster dates.

7. **Digital Vaccination Certificate:** Owners can generate a downloadable PDF vaccination certificate containing verified records and the pet’s QR code.

8. **Lost Pet Mode & QR Scan Alert:** Owners can mark a pet as lost. Anyone scanning the QR code can contact the owner and optionally share the pet’s found location.

9. **Community Stray & CNVR Tracking:** - Rescue organizations can record community dogs, vaccination status, sterilization status, identification marks, photos, and release locations.

10. **Duplicate Stray Detection:** The system will compare existing records using location, physical appearance, identification marks, tags, and other attributes to reduce duplicate CNVR records.

11. **Animal Bite & Suspected Rabies Reporting:** - Users can report incidents with severity, description, images, date/time, animal information, and GPS location.

12. **Geospatial Incident Mapping:** Bite incidents, suspected-rabies reports, community dogs, and CNVR activities will be displayed through an interactive map.

13. **Risk Area & Incident Cluster Detection:** Spatial analysis will identify areas where multiple bite or suspected-rabies reports are concentrated.

14. **Nearby Vet & Shelter Finder:** - Users can find registered veterinary clinics, shelters, and rescue organizations near their current or selected location.

15. **AI Assistance / Chatbot:** An AI chatbot can help users navigate the system, understand available features, locate relevant services, and obtain general guidance about pet care and what actions to take after an animal-related incident. It should provide guidance only and not diagnose rabies or replace medical/veterinary professionals.

16. **Nepali & English Support:** The application will support both languages to improve accessibility for users across Nepal.

17. Chat between - Pet owner, Vet

18. **Stray Dog Digital Identity / Re-identification** — when a rescue team finds a community dog, they upload its photo and location; the system searches previous records to determine whether the same dog may already have been vaccinated/sterilized, instead of relying only on manual duplicate checking.
