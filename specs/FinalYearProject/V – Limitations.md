This section explains the realistic boundaries of **RabiesTrack Nepal** and what the first FYP version may not fully achieve.

- **Prototype Scope:** The system will initially be developed as a prototype for selected municipalities or urban areas rather than as a nationwide platform.
- **Dependence on User-Submitted Data:** Incident, stray, lost-pet, and location records may depend on the accuracy of information provided by users or organizations.
- **AI Breed Prediction Accuracy:** Breed detection may be less accurate for mixed breeds, poor-quality images, uncommon breeds, or visually similar breeds.
- **No Medical Diagnosis:** The platform and chatbot will not diagnose rabies or any other disease and will not replace professional medical or veterinary consultation.
- **Unverified Public Reports:** Bite or suspected-rabies reports submitted by the public may initially represent reported incidents rather than medically confirmed rabies cases.
- **Limited Institutional Integration:** The FYP version may not directly integrate with existing government, hospital, municipal, or national veterinary information systems.
- **Notification Dependency:** SMS, email, or other alerts may depend on third-party services, internet connectivity, and available service credits.
- **Internet Requirement:** Most functions will require internet access, particularly maps, AI features, notifications, and cloud-based services.
- **Location Accuracy:** GPS coordinates may vary depending on device accuracy, user permission, and network conditions.
- **Incomplete Animal Population Data:** The system may not know the total dog population of a ward, so it should avoid presenting unsupported vaccination-coverage percentages.
- **Duplicate Detection Limitations:** Similar-looking stray dogs may still be incorrectly matched or missed, so duplicate detection should assist human review rather than automatically make final decisions.
- **Data Availability:** Useful analytics depend on having enough reliable records. Early-stage maps or cluster detection may be less meaningful when only a small amount of data exists.
- **Time Constraint:** Since the project is being developed within a limited FYP timeframe, some advanced features may be simplified, postponed, or moved to future development.

The key point is that these limitations do **not weaken the project**. They show that the scope is realistic and that you understand where human verification, external systems, or future development are still required.
