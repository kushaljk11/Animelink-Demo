### Project Aim

The aim of **Rabies Track Nepal** is to design and develop a centralized web-based platform that improves pet vaccination management, animal identification, community stray tracking, and rabies-related monitoring in Nepal through digital records, automation, and geospatial technologies.

### Project Objectives

The project aims to:

1. Develop a secure role-based platform for pet owners, veterinary clinics, rescue organizations, and ward/municipal officers.
2. Create digital pet profiles with QR-based identification and accessible vaccination information.
3. Allow verified veterinary clinics to record and maintain trusted vaccination records.
4. Automatically track vaccination status and send reminders for upcoming or overdue boosters.
5. Support community stray and CNVR tracking with location and identification information.
6. Enable users to report animal-bite and suspected-rabies incidents with GPS location and supporting details.
7. Use geospatial mapping and analysis to identify incident clusters and potentially higher-risk areas.
8. Provide nearby veterinary clinic, shelter, and rescue organization discovery through map-based search.
9. Support AI-assisted features such as pet breed prediction and an informational chatbot.
10. Provide bilingual support in **Nepali and English** for better accessibility.
11. Allow authorized users to generate and export useful reports for monitoring and analysis.
12. Build the system in a way that allows additional relevant features to be added later after further research, brainstorming, requirement analysis, and supervisor feedback.
