RabiesTrack Nepal connects different stakeholders through one centralized platform.

1. Pet owners register their pets and create a digital health profile with a unique QR code.
2. Veterinary clinics verify and update vaccination records, including rabies and other important vaccines.
3. The system automatically tracks booster dates and sends reminders before vaccinations expire.
4. Animal shelters and rescue organizations record stray dogs, including vaccination and CNVR/sterilization details.
5. Citizens can report animal bites or suspected-rabies incidents with location information.
6. The system maps these reports geographically and helps identify areas with more incidents or possible risk clusters.
7. Ward or municipal authorities can view summarized data and maps to support monitoring and planning.

In simple flow:

Register → Verify → Track → Remind → Report → Map → Analyze
