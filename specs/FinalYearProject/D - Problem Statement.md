Right now, the project is built around a few connected problems: pet vaccination records can be lost or fragmented, booster dates can be missed, stray vaccination and sterilization activities may not be centrally tracked, and bite incidents are usually handled individually without a shared geospatial record that can show possible high-risk areas.

Rabies Track Nepal tries to bring these pieces together instead of solving only one small problem.

###### **Why is it different from others**

The project is not just a pet-management system and not just a vaccination tracker.

Its stronger idea is the combination of:

Digital Pet Identity + Verified Vaccination Records + Automated Reminders + CNVR Tracking + Bite Incident Mapping + Geospatial Analytics
