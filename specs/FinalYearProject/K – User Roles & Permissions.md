### 1. Pet Owner

Pet owners can:

- Create and manage their account
- Register and manage their pets
- View digital pet profiles
- View vaccination history and status
- Receive vaccination and booster reminders
- Generate digital vaccination certificates
- Access and share their pet QR code
- Mark a pet as lost
- Receive QR scan/found-location alerts
- Report animal-bite or suspected-rabies incidents
- Find nearby veterinary clinics and shelters
- Use the AI breed finder
- Use the AI chatbot for general guidance

### 2. Veterinary Clinic

Verified veterinary clinics can:

- Manage clinic information
- Search or access registered pet records where permitted
- Add and verify vaccination records
- Record vaccine name, batch number, vaccination date, and booster date
- Update relevant pet medical information
- Confirm vaccination status
- Generate or validate vaccination certificates
- View relevant reports and history

### 3. Rescue Organization / Shelter

Authorized rescue organizations can:

- Register community or stray animals
- Maintain CNVR records
- Record vaccination and sterilization details
- Add animal photos, identification marks, and release locations
- Check for possible duplicate stray records
- Manage relevant shelter/rescue information
- View mapped stray and CNVR activity
- Access relevant incident information where permitted

### 4. Ward / Municipal Officer

Authorized ward or municipal officers can:

- View animal-bite and suspected-rabies reports
- Access geospatial incident maps
- View incident clusters and higher-risk areas
- Monitor CNVR and vaccination activities
- Filter information by location, ward, date, or status
- View statistical summaries and dashboards
- Export authorized reports in PDF or CSV format
- Update or manage incident status where applicable

### 5. Public / Guest User

A public user may have limited access without creating an account.

They can:

- Scan a pet QR code and view selected public information
- Access emergency owner contact details where permitted
- View lost-pet information
- Submit limited bite or suspected-rabies reports if allowed
- Find nearby registered vets, shelters, or rescue organizations

### Permission Principle

Rabies Track Nepal will follow **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)**.

Each user will only be able to access information and actions relevant to their assigned role.

For example:

**Pet Owner → Own Pet Data**  
**Vet Clinic → Vaccination & Clinical Records**  
**Rescue Organization → Stray/CNVR Management**  
**Ward Officer → Monitoring, Mapping & Reports**

This helps keep the system **secure, organized, and privacy-aware**.
