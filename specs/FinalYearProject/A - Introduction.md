Name - Rabies Track Nepal
Title - Rabies Track Nepal: A Web-Based Pet Immunization and Geospatial Rabies Surveillance System

##### Short Introduction

Rabies Track Nepal is a web-based platform designed to improve pet vaccination management, animal identification, community stray tracking, and rabies-related surveillance in Nepal. The system connects pet owners, veterinary clinics, animal welfare organizations, and local authorities through a shared digital platform.

The project focuses on replacing fragmented or paper-based animal health records with digital pet profiles, QR-based identification, verified vaccination records, automated booster reminders, stray/CNVR tracking, and location-based reporting of animal bites or suspected rabies cases.

A major part of the system is its geospatial capability. Reported incidents can be mapped and analyzed by location, helping identify areas with higher concentrations of bite incidents or suspected rabies activity. This can support better monitoring, planning, and coordination at ward or municipal level.

The project is being designed primarily for the Nepalese context and follows a One Health-oriented approach by connecting animal health, community management, and public-health-related information in one system.
