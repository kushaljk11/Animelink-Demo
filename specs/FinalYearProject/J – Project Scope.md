Rabies Track Nepal will focus on the following areas:

- User registration and role-based access
- Pet registration and digital pet profiles
- AI-assisted pet breed identification
- QR-based pet identification
- Veterinarian-verified vaccination records
- Vaccination status tracking and automated reminders
- Digital vaccination certificate generation
- Lost pet mode and QR scan alerts
- Community stray and CNVR tracking
- Duplicate stray record detection
- Animal-bite and suspected-rabies reporting
- GPS-based incident recording
- Geospatial mapping of incidents, strays, and CNVR activities
- Risk-area and incident-cluster detection
- Nearby veterinary clinic and shelter finder
- AI chatbot for general system and pet-health guidance
- PDF/CSV report export
- Nepali and English language support

### Target Scope

The system will initially be developed as a **prototype for selected municipalities or urban areas of Nepal**, rather than as a nationwide government system.

The project will mainly demonstrate how **digital animal-health records, vaccination tracking, community stray management, automation, and geospatial analysis** can work together within one platform.

### Scope Flexibility

The current features represent the planned initial scope.

Additional relevant features may be added later after further **research, brainstorming, requirement analysis, prototype testing, and discussion with the project supervisor**, provided they are achievable within the available FYP timeframe.

### Out of Scope

For the initial FYP version, the system will not:

- Diagnose rabies or other medical conditions
- Replace professional veterinary or medical advice
- Replace Nepal's official government health-surveillance systems
- Provide nationwide real-time rabies statistics unless official data sources are available
- Guarantee the identity or medical condition of an animal based only on AI image analysis
- Act as an emergency medical service
