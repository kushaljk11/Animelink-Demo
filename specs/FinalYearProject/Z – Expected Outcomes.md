By the end of the project, **Rabies Track Nepal** is expected to produce a working prototype that demonstrates how pet-health management, stray/CNVR tracking, and rabies-related geospatial monitoring can be integrated into one system.

Expected outcomes include:

- A functional web application with role-based access
- Digital pet profiles with QR-based identification
- Verified vaccination records and vaccination-status tracking
- Automated booster reminders
- Downloadable digital vaccination certificates
- Lost-pet mode with QR scan support
- Community stray and CNVR tracking
- Duplicate stray detection support
- Animal-bite and suspected-rabies reporting
- Interactive geospatial maps
- Incident cluster and higher-risk area visualization
- Nearby vet and shelter discovery
- AI-assisted breed prediction
- AI chatbot support
- Nepali and English language support
- PDF/CSV report export
- Responsive design for desktop, tablet, and mobile
- Secure handling of user, pet, and location information

### Overall Expected Result

The final outcome should demonstrate that a centralized digital system can make animal-health information easier to **record, verify, track, map, analyze, and communicate**.

It should also show the technical feasibility of combining:

**Full-Stack Web Development + Geospatial Analysis + Background Automation + QR Technology + AI Assistance + Role-Based Access**

within a practical Nepal-focused FYP.
