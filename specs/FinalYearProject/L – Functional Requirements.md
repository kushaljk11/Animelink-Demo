For Rabies Track Nepal, you can document them like this:

1. The system shall allow users to register and log in securely.
2. The system shall provide role-based access for pet owners, veterinary clinics, rescue organizations, ward/municipal officers, and public users where applicable.
3. The system shall allow pet owners to register, update, and manage pet profiles.
4. The system shall allow users to upload a pet image and receive an AI-based breed suggestion.
5. The system shall generate a unique QR code for each registered pet.
6. The system shall display selected public pet information when the QR code is scanned.
7. The system shall allow verified veterinary clinics to add and manage vaccination records.
8. The system shall store vaccine details such as vaccine name, batch number, administered date, and next booster date.
9. The system shall automatically determine vaccination status as Up to Date, Due Soon, or Overdue.
10. The system shall send vaccination and booster reminders to pet owners.
11. The system shall generate downloadable digital vaccination certificates.
12. The system shall allow owners to mark pets as lost.
13. The system shall allow a finder to contact the owner after scanning the pet QR code.
14. The system shall optionally allow the finder to share the found location of a lost pet.
15. The system shall allow authorized rescue organizations to register community or stray animals.
16. The system shall allow CNVR, vaccination, sterilization, and release-location records to be maintained.
17. The system shall check existing stray records to help identify possible duplicates.
18. The system shall allow users to report animal-bite and suspected-rabies incidents.
19. The system shall record incident information including description, severity, image, date/time, and GPS location.
20. The system shall display incidents, stray records, and CNVR activities on an interactive map.
21. The system shall support spatial analysis for detecting incident concentrations or clusters.
22. The system shall allow users to find nearby registered veterinary clinics, shelters, and rescue organizations.
23. The system shall provide an AI chatbot for general system guidance and pet-health information.
24. The system shall allow authorized users to filter and export relevant reports in PDF or CSV format.
25. The system shall support both Nepali and English.
26. The system shall allow authorized ward or municipal users to view dashboards and monitoring information relevant to their area.
27. The system shall maintain appropriate access permissions so users can only perform actions allowed by their role.

For your documentation, this section should stay fairly objective and technical. It should describe **what the system does**, not how you will implement it.
