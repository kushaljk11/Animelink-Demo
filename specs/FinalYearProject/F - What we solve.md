We solve the **fragmentation and tracking problem** around pet health and rabies-related information in Nepal.

In simple terms, Rabies Track Nepal helps solve:

- Lost or scattered pet vaccination records
- Missed rabies and booster vaccination dates
- Difficulty verifying a pet’s vaccination history
- Poor coordination between pet owners, vets, rescue groups, and local authorities
- Lack of organized CNVR/stray dog tracking
- Animal-bite and suspected-rabies reports not being analyzed geographically
- Difficulty identifying higher-risk areas or incident clusters

So the core problem we solve is:

**“Animal-health and rabies-related information is scattered and difficult to track, verify, and analyze. Rabies Track Nepal brings it into one connected digital system.”**
